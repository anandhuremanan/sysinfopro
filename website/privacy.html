Privacy policy

SysInfo Pro by Probably Fine Labs · Last updated 14 October 2026

SysInfo Pro is a system information and maintenance tool for Windows. It runs entirely on your PC. Probably Fine Labs does not collect, receive or store any information about you or your computer. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no telemetry.

What the app reads on your PC

To show its information, the app reads details about your computer: hardware, drives and free space, running programs, network ports, startup programs, installed software, PATH entries, battery and security settings. This information is shown to you and is not sent anywhere.

What the app saves on your PC

  • Settings such as your theme and which readings the widget shows.
  • Snapshots for "What changed": lists of startup programs, installed software, listening ports and PATH entries, kept so the app can show you what changed. They are stored in your user folder under AppData\Roaming\SysInfoPro, and the app keeps at most the last 20.
  • Files you choose to export, such as the spec card image and the system report, are saved to your Downloads folder. The system report includes your device's serial number; check it before sharing it.

Uninstalling the app, or deleting those folders, removes this data.

Services the app contacts

Some features need the internet. When they run, your PC connects directly to the services below, which receive your IP address as part of any internet connection. Each service's own privacy policy applies.

  • Speed test (only when you start one): Cloudflare (speed.cloudflare.com and 1.1.1.1). If Cloudflare is unavailable, Measurement Lab (M-Lab) is used instead. M-Lab publishes all test results, including the IP address and the measured speeds, as open data for internet research.
  • Internet connection check: a brief connection to Google's public DNS server (8.8.8.8) to see whether you are online. No data is sent.
  • Software version check (only when you open the Software tool): the app asks the publishers of common developer tools for their latest version numbers, from nodejs.org, go.dev, endoflife.date and GitHub. It does not send them your list of installed software.
  • Update check (versions downloaded from our website only): the app reads the list of released versions from GitHub. Versions installed from the Microsoft Store do not do this; the Store handles their updates.

Links you choose to open, such as a program's download page, open in your web browser.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed.

Contact
